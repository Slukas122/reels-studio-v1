# Neutral default design system: native Reel

Use this only when no approved brand or useful reference exists. It is an unbranded production fallback, not an invented client identity. Do not add a logo or brand name. Record any changes in the job's style sheet so later videos in a series remain coherent. For footage-led work, the camera image and speaker are the design; avoid a television-news look.

## Visual language

| Role | Default | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Image | Full-bleed source footage in 9:16 | Preserve the person, action and evidence. Crop intentionally; avoid a designed background around ordinary talking-head footage. |
| Captions | White, readable sans with a restrained dark outline or shadow | Short groups close to the speech. Keep them inside the tested shared safe area. Use a small translucent backing only when the footage defeats contrast. |
| Accent | One subtle warm yellow `#FFE07A` | Highlight a meaning-bearing word sparingly. A video can have no accent. Replace if footage or subject calls for it. |
| Typography | A licensed sans family with Czech diacritics, e.g. Inter or system equivalent | Medium or bold captions; avoid all-caps blocks and oversized editorial headlines by default. Use real font metrics. |
| Graphics | Small labels or simple in-frame callouts only where they add information | No permanent frame, ticker, news-style lower third, stacked boxes, default logo or decorative counters. |
| Motion | Direct cuts, occasional motivated punch-in or quick type entrance | Keep each frame simple but let timing carry energy. No permanent camera drift or mandatory movement cycle. |
| Sound | Clear speech first; rhythmic licensed music when it helps | Match prominent visual changes to audible accents in motion-only work. In spoken work, the voice sets the pace. |

For a conventional talking-head Reel, use speech and captions as the default visual system. Start on a compelling source shot, not a title card. Keep the picture visible through most of the runtime. Minimal does not mean slow: tighten thought boundaries, choose strong reactions or proof shots and time concise caption changes well. Archival reporting may need a quiet date or location label; energetic product work may use a brighter accent and faster motion. Never alter the color of source footage simply to fit a palette. Keep a phone-size reading test in the style sheet.

## System behavior

Use at most three levels: main idea, supporting detail, quiet source/context. A hook card, chapter card or CTA is optional and must solve a real communication need. For footage-led Reels, a short in-frame title or ordinary subtitle is usually enough. Motion-only explainers and ads may use fuller typography and graphic composition, with a single strong visual job per beat; the visible sequence must convey the offer and proof even when silent. Follow [minimal-rhythm.md](minimal-rhythm.md) for the different motion density of these formats. When an actual client manual arrives, replace these defaults with it and preserve any explicitly requested creative choice.
