# Native short-form playbook: evidence to editing decisions

This is a decision aid, not a recipe for views. It comes from a purposive review of seven public Instagram Reels by Duolingo, MKBHD, Ryanair, Gary Vaynerchuk, Liquid Death and Neil Patel (October 2026). The reviewed files, source links and detailed observations are in the job's benchmark report. There were no comparable retention curves; do not claim that a particular cut, caption or effect caused their reach. Treat transfer to TikTok and Facebook as a hypothesis to verify with platform previews and the client's own results.

## First decide what the viewer is watching for

| Viewer wants to... | Open with | Hold or cut when | Graphics and ending |
|---|---|---|---|
| Understand an expert's point | The strongest truthful source sentence, exchange or specific topic | Cut when the argument advances, a repetition ends or a useful example begins; preserve qualifiers and speaker intent | Short timed captions. Add a term, real screenshot or verified figure only at the relevant claim. End on the resolved thought. |
| See whether a tool or product works | The real product, output, task or result in action | Give each interaction enough time to read; use the same conditions for comparisons; cut to evidence when it answers the spoken question | Small step labels, cursor emphasis or a comparison if needed. Keep the actual UI and result visible. |
| Get a situational joke or reaction | The situation in one concise line or a real action | Hold setup long enough to register; change on reaction or punchline, even if that means one long shot | Minimal text. No compulsory logo or explanatory end card that weakens the joke. Use third-party clips only with rights. |
| Evaluate an offer or campaign | Product or clear situation, then a concrete benefit or tension | Advance with a change of scene, speaker, action or offer; maintain enough time for the reveal | A stronger art direction, music and motion can carry the story. Identify the product and offer accurately. CTA only when it serves the brief. |
| Follow a motion-only idea | A visible proposition, object or transformation | Change on an intelligible visual progression and musical phrase | Use kinetic type and designed transitions freely, but each frame needs one legible job and a claim supported by the brief. |

When a source could fit multiple rows, choose the row that carries the **actual main idea** and supporting evidence. Do not turn a self-worth dialogue into a marketing tip merely because its creator works in marketing. Do not turn a technology demonstration into an abstract AI montage.

## Source-to-cut test

1. Read the complete source and timecoded transcript; listen to uncertain words. Write one central idea and two or three indispensable subpoints with source ranges. For a silent clip, map visible actions and verified context instead.
2. Write the first beat as `what the viewer sees/hears -> what it promises -> exact payoff source/time`. Use a question only if the footage answers it. Prefer a product, result, real exchange or concrete statement over generic suspense.
3. For every proposed cut, write its job: remove repetition, change idea, reveal evidence, change speaker, deliver reaction or match a real action. If a shot's evidence needs reading time, let it run. Captions or movement within the shot can carry rhythm.
4. For every graphic, write `confusion -> visual answer -> supporting source`. If the visual only decorates, remove it. A real screen, output or comparison usually beats decorative network nodes for AI, technology and marketing topics. Never fabricate a platform UI, metric or result.
5. Read captions at phone size with speech muted. Start with a restrained sans-serif style, short semantic phrases (often 2–5 words), high contrast and little or no box. Tune line length and display time to the language and pace; the number is a starting point, not a forced segmentation rule. Caption **actual final audio**, not a cleaned-up paraphrase.
6. Listen to the export. Speech has priority. Music establishes energy only when helpful; SFX mark a meaningful action or reveal. Do not automate a whoosh on every cut. A music-led fashion or motion spot may use few or no spoken captions.
7. End on the point or product reveal. Add a logo, follow prompt or CTA only when the user objective and content call for it. A final card is optional, especially for organic expert, demo and meme formats.

## Platform and measurement checks

- For a shared Instagram, Facebook and TikTok export, start with 9:16 and place important text, face, product and UI outside the app controls using [platform-safe-zones.md](platform-safe-zones.md). Test the **encoded final file** under each relevant interface mask. Make placement-specific layouts when captions or controls obstruct the common version. The source sample includes a 4:5 video; its existence is not a reason to default to 4:5.
- Do not impose a universal cut interval or a universal first-three-second gimmick. The reviewed set included a 7.5-second meme with roughly one detectable cut, a 60-second hands-on demonstration with about sixteen and a 47-second scripted ad with about seven. Scene detection is approximate and can miss jump cuts.
- When improving a series, test alternative **truthful** openings on the client's own audience. Compare first-seconds retention, average watch time, completion, saves and relevant visits or leads as appropriate. Record the topic and distribution conditions before attributing a change to the edit.

## Study anchors

- [MKBHD airless basketball](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C3ifVLPLMLW/): object in opening, explicit question, physical demonstration and payoff.
- [Neil Patel social media SEO](https://www.instagram.com/p/DTSnPn5CWzX/): direct topic, real platform imagery where discussed, short captions. Its numerical claims were not independently verified.
- That Neil Patel example also shows a **promise gap**: the opening says "how to master" the topic, while the rest mostly argues why it matters. Do not copy that gap. Either deliver the promised steps or write a more accurate opening.
- [Gary Vaynerchuk dialogue](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DMpuiXeubFY/): human exchange to larger point, minimal graphic treatment.
- [Ryanair situational meme](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C1Z7-xZpewe/): setup can hold without frequent cuts.
- [Liquid Death scripted ad](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DOL68uxEh95/): changes on dialogue and product reveal; its 4:5 frame and innuendo are brand-specific.
- [Duolingo fashion promo](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdEZ8ZERpPs/): music-led art direction and branded resolution belong to a campaign, not every talking-head Reel.

Platform references: [Meta Reels ad guidance](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads), [TikTok safe-zone specification](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/tiktok-reservation-in-feed-ads-reach-frequency?lang=en-0). Platform UI changes; verify the live placement instead of relying on a fixed pixel margin.
